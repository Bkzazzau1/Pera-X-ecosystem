# Pera-X Token Allocation Structure

## Core Token Parameters

```text
Token: Pera-X
Total Supply: 1,000,000,000 Pera-X
Supply Policy: Fixed supply
Initial Price: $0.0000264
Initial Fully Diluted Valuation: $26,400
Network: Solana
```

The total supply is fixed. No future minting should be introduced unless the project governance formally changes the token policy.

## Allocation Summary

| Category | Allocation | Token Amount |
|---|---:|---:|
| Liquidity Pool | 38% | 380,000,000 |
| Community / Utility Rewards | 17% | 170,000,000 |
| Treasury | 12% | 120,000,000 |
| Ecosystem / Marketing | 12% | 120,000,000 |
| Trading Company Operations & Revenue Settlement | 7% | 70,000,000 |
| Team | 6% | 60,000,000 |
| Private / Strategic Investors | 5% | 50,000,000 |
| Advisors | 3% | 30,000,000 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **1,000,000,000** |

## 1. Liquidity Pool — 38%

This allocation will support strong DEX liquidity, reduce volatility, improve trading confidence, and build trust at launch.

## 2. Community / Utility Rewards — 17%

This will support user rewards, referral bonuses, service discounts, loyalty incentives, and community growth for users who actively use Pera-X for calls, virtual numbers, data, bills, and other utilities.

## 3. Treasury — 12%

This will be reserved for future development, strategic expansion, emergency support, listings, integrations, audits, and long-term sustainability of the Pera-X ecosystem.

## 4. Ecosystem / Marketing — 12%

This allocation will support partnerships, campaigns, influencer marketing, exchange/DEX awareness, product adoption, brand growth, and ecosystem expansion.

## 5. Trading Company Operations & Revenue Settlement — 7%

This allocation will support the trading company’s operational role, including service settlement, liquidity support, buyback-and-burn execution, treasury strengthening, liquidity support, and utility expansion.

This allocation will be released gradually and used only for real business operations.

## 6. Team — 6%

This is allocated to the founding and core development team. It will be vested gradually to show long-term commitment and avoid early selling pressure.

## 7. Private / Strategic Investors — 5%

This allocation is reserved for early strategic investors who support funding, partnerships, liquidity, infrastructure, and growth.

Investor tokens will be subject to gradual vesting.

## 8. Advisors — 3%

This is reserved for advisors who provide technical, legal, tokenomics, telecom, fintech, exchange, and strategic support.

Advisor tokens will be vested gradually.

## Launch Interpretation

At the initial price of $0.0000264 with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 Pera-X, the implied fully diluted valuation is:

```text
1,000,000,000 × $0.0000264 = $26,400
```

This low initial valuation supports early community participation while allowing the project to grow through real utility demand, DEX liquidity, payment activity, buyback-and-burn policy, and trading company revenue settlement.
